Thresholds

Deviation is the average distance between the stroke you drew and the reference, as a fraction of the character's box — 0.10 means a tenth of the character's width away, on average. The original values came from perturbing KanjiVG's own strokes with noise, which described a plotter rather than a hand: the old shape limit was 0.094, so ordinary handwriting failed.

Order and direction are decided by comparison, not by an absolute limit — but the order guard only applies to strokes already under the shape limit, so a shape limit that is too tight lets false "out of order" through as well.
A stroke closer than this is simply right.
Beyond this it is a different stroke, not a wobbly one. Between the two reads as "off-shape".
